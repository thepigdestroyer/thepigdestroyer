# Mrinal Joshi

Research Assistant in the Skene Lab (neurogenomics) at the UK Dementia Research Institute, Imperial College London. I build reproducible bioinformatics pipelines, AI agents for biology and developer tools.

Website: [mrinal.is-a.dev](https://mrinal.is-a.dev/)

## Projects

- [deRoute](https://drouter-demo.vercel.app/): a private AI router that checks the sealed hardware before a prompt leaves your browser. Won the Agents That Ship track at Encode London 2026, built solo.
- [pegRNA designer](https://pegrna-designer.vercel.app/): design pegRNAs for C-terminal protein tagging by prime editing. Open to everyone, no sign-in.
- [Positive Control](https://m-lab.is-an.ai/): a journal and skills map for agentic biology.
- [FLAIR-AD](https://flair-ad.netlify.app/): companion site for a proposed PhD on how IRF1 binding switches microglia into disease-associated states in Alzheimer's disease.
- [A day at the bench](https://mj-forge.is-an.ai/): a lab game with qPCR, CRISPR gene tagging, pipetting and fluorescence microscopy.
- [Hearth](https://hearth.is-an.ai/): an AI agent for London rental search.
- [AI Studio](https://m-joshi.ai.studio/): playable demos of my projects.
